Origin Story
"I drove 2 hours to Rumney. The rock was wet. The weather app said 0% rain."

That's it. That's the whole story. I'm Frank — I climb outdoors in New England, which means I play a constant gamble with microclimate weather. Standard forecast apps don't know what granite friction feels like after morning fog. They don't know that north-facing walls seep for days after rain. Climbers know. But there was no app that asked them.

So I built one. CodClimb uses the Open-Meteo API to pull hyper-local weather data for any crag, then runs it through a climbing-specific scoring algorithm — accounting for humidity, dew point, recent precipitation, wind, and temperature — to output a single number: your Climb Score. 0 to 100.

Then the community layer kicks in. Real climbers post real-time reports. They earn reputation with every post. That's the data weather APIs will never have.

The Problem

Weather apps give city-level forecasts. Crags are in canyons, on cliff faces, in forests. They dry differently. They seep differently. A 0% rain forecast means nothing when the rock is seeping from two days ago.

The Solution

A climbing-specific weather score + live community reports. Data where it matters: the actual wall, the actual moment, from people who are actually there.

The Market

7.7M climbers in the US. Most climb outdoors at some point. No dominant app solved the "is my crag climbable right now?" problem with community data — so in June 2026, CodClimb went live on the App Store to be it.

Features

Everything you need before you leave the car

🎯
0–100 Climbability Score

One number per crag, built from five weighted factors — temperature, dryness, humidity, wind, and cloud cover. Fully transparent: tap any factor for a plain-English breakdown of what's driving the score.

🗺️
Score Map & Smart Filters

All 237 crags on a live map with color-coded score pins. Filter by discipline — sport, trad, boulder, or ice — plus rock type, sun exposure, and region. Green means go, red means rest day.

🧗
Live Community Reports

Real climbers post real-time conditions with up to 4 photos — seepage, dry sectors, crowds, fixed gear. Threaded comments and thumbs-up float the best beta to the top.

📈
7-Day & Hourly Forecast

Hour-by-hour crag scores, a full weather meteogram, rain-timing detail, drying countdown, and a draggable sun/shade timeline computed from each wall's aspect. Time your send.

🔔
Trip Planner & Alerts

Arm a trip and get a day-before briefing, a morning-of update, and a push alert if rain is about to hit the crag. Or set a score threshold on your home crag and get pinged the moment it turns good.

⚖️
Scoring That Fits You

Tune each factor's weight to your style, set your personal temperature comfort, and pick sun or shade preference. Mixed crags score Exposed and Overhang walls separately — overhangs win on wet days.
